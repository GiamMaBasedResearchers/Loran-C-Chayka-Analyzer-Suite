A band is dark when the PEAK pixel-column activity inside it is near zero (true black gap); dashed traces keep the band active. Overrides are hard switches applied everywhere: on:N forces the station present (bypasses the presence gates), dark:N forces NO SIGNAL. Formats: 19, 19-23, 19,20 (commas, spaces and + are all accepted).

Detection (noise & day/night robust)

A station is flagged NO SIGNAL if its peak is below the presence gate OR its duty cycle after the first ON is below "Min duty" (real stations: 30–100%; neighbor bleed: a few %). Overrides on:/dark: bypass these gates.

Night shading (solar)

Night = sun elevation < −6° at the receiver site (default: DL0AO Munich).
